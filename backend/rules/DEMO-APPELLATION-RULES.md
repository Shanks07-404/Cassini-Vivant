## § 1.1 — Authorized Active Frost Protection Methods

Vineyard operators within the illustrative appellation may deploy the following active frost protection measures during radiative spring frost events:

1. **Water sprinkling (over-vine and under-vine):** Exploits latent heat release during water freezing to maintain bud temperature near 0°C through continuous irrigation.
2. **Frost candles and paraffin pots:** Controlled combustion of wax or paraffin placed between vine rows at recommended density levels appropriate to the site.
3. **Wind machines (mobile and fixed):** Mechanical mixing of warm air from the elevated inversion layer back down to the vineyard canopy during temperature inversion nights.
4. **Heated cables on training wires:** Targeted electrical heating of the fruiting canes (limited to experimental installations).

---

## § 1.2 — Activation Window & Phenological Stages

Active intervention is framed by the vine growth cycle:

- **Reference phenological stage:** Protection is justified from bud swell onward and becomes critical once green tip is visible.
- **Alert threshold:** Shelter temperature at bud level falling to or below the frost damage threshold for the cultivar.
- **Economic precaution:** Activation of heavy equipment (sprinkling, candles) incurs a cost per parcel per night. Intervention should be prioritized according to calibrated local frost probability relative to the cost-loss action threshold.

---

## § 1.3 — Collective Resource Sharing & Priority Parcels

Valley floors and topographic hollows accumulate cold air through katabatic drainage. Parcels below a site-specific relative elevation threshold are designated as "Severe Inversion Risk Parcels."

When water or labor resources are limited, the appellation authority may order priority deployment on parcels with the highest viticultural value located in topographic cold pools.

---

## § 1.4 — Reporting & Traceability

All frost protection activations must be:

1. Declared to the monitoring system at least two hours before operational deployment.
2. Recorded in the farm log with start time, end time, protected parcels, and resource consumption.
